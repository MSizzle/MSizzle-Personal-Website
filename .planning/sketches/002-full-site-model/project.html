MAHealth
Scanner

Work · 2026 · AI · Health

MAHealth Scanner reads a consumer product and gives you a fast, honest read on how healthy it really is. Point it at a label or a barcode and it cuts through the marketing to a simple verdict.

The hard part was trust. A health score is worthless if people cannot see why it landed where it did, so the tool always shows its reasoning: which ingredients moved the needle, and how much.

What it does

Parses product data, scores it against nutrition and additive heuristics, and returns a plain-language summary you can act on in the aisle.
