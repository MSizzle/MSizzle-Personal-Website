AI is Nibbling
the World

5 min read · June 2026 · Technology

Software was supposed to eat the world in one big bite. Instead, AI is nibbling. A task here, a workflow there, the boring middle of a job quietly handed off while nobody throws a party about it.

That changes how you should think about building. The question is no longer whether a tool can do the whole job. It is which small, annoying, repeated piece it can take, and how cleanly it hands the rest back to you.

The winners will not be the people who automate everything. They will be the people who notice, faster than anyone else, which nibble is worth taking.

The boring middle

Most work is a sandwich. There is judgment at the start, judgment at the end, and a thick layer of mechanical sameness in between. AI is very good at the middle and still clumsy at the edges. That is the opportunity, and also the discipline.

So I build narrow. One pipeline that turns a PDF into a presentation. One agent that drafts the follow-up. Each one small enough to trust, and small enough to throw away when something better arrives.

What this means for builders

Stay close to the work. The best automations come from watching a real person do a real task and flinching at the part that wastes their afternoon. That flinch is the spec.

The world is not getting eaten in one dramatic moment. It is getting nibbled, steadily, by people paying attention. I would rather be one of them.
