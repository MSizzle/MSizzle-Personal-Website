<objective>
Four independent, atomic-commit fixes to the Notion image pipeline, diagnosed live by the
orchestrator (do not re-diagnose): blank/blotchy pinboard image pops, weak proxy caching that
re-hits Notion and re-runs sharp on every cold edge miss, double image optimization on the
card/cover path (Next's /_next/image re-encoding an already-sharp-processed proxy response), and
pinboard covers always pulling the proxy's 640px default into 150-210px slots.

Purpose: faster, smoother image loads across the card grid, blog/project cover hero, and the
"Things I Love" pinboard, without touching layout, drag/shuffle/draw behavior (TL-01), or the
pure-mono visual language.

Output: four commits, one per fix, in this order (cheapest/lowest-risk first, riskiest last):
1. Hard proxy caching (notion-cover + notion-image routes) -- pure header change, smallest blast radius.
2. Fade-in + placeholder (pinboard fade/dimensions, card-cover + newsletter blur) -- the highest
   visible win, still low risk (additive props/state only).
3. Right-size delivery (pinboard retina ?w= sizing) -- depends on the FRAME_SIZES map Task 2 introduces.
4. Stop double-optimizing (unoptimized on every next/image wrapping /api/notion-cover) -- the
   riskiest because it changes an existing, working image-loading mechanism across four files, so
   it is reasoned through last and most carefully, after the safer wins are already locked in.

Explicitly OUT of scope (per the calling orchestrator): precomputing/caching covers into Vercel
Blob storage. Do not add that or any other "bigger lever."
