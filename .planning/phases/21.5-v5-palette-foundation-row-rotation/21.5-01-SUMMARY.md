## Decisions Made
- Ink set to `#111111` rather than `#000000` per the locked spec (halation on a warm ground; visually indistinguishable from `#1A1A18` in Monty's comparison).
- Ochre locked at `#8E6214` (4.81:1); the originally-considered `#9A6A16` (4.22:1) explicitly fails AA and is guarded against by name in the test (`9A6A16` must appear nowhere in the comment-stripped CSS).
- Accent tokens declared outside `@theme inline` to avoid Tailwind's utility generation and tree-shaking risk, since their only consumer is a future inline `style` attribute (21.5-02).
