system: bone `#F5F2EB` ground, near-black `#111111` ink, every derived value re-derived from that

that does not exist, and the row hover text cannot flip to bone until `--color-text-inverse` is
bone. Nothing in this plan applies an accent to anything -- it only makes the palette available.

`src/__tests__/styles/v5-tokens.test.ts` that fails the moment any of it drifts.
