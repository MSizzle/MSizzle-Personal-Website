<decisions>
## Implementation Decisions

### Aesthetic (LOCKED)
- **D-01:** KEEP the current v3 look — the existing Pumpkin Amber palette and current fonts. This is a STRUCTURE/IA rebuild in the existing skin, NOT a restyle. The Prometheus "flame" palette + Fraunces/Space Grotesk type were mocked (`.planning/sketches/006-flame-personal-brand/mock.html`) and Monty REJECTED both. Do NOT re-introduce them.
- **D-02:** Reuse the existing v3 design tokens and editorial/primitive components. Do not add new color tokens or fonts.
- **D-03 [REC — iteration lever, not blocking]:** A permissible light touch is dialing the orange from a full background field down to an accent (keep palette, reduce saturation of the field). Default for the first pass: keep the current treatment; treat orange-intensity as something Monty tunes after seeing the page live. Do not block the build on this.

### Narrative & structure
- **D-04:** The homepage is a single narrative arc — **who am I → what I'm about / building → how to engage** — as a *feel*, not three rigid labeled sections.
- **D-05:** Remove the WebGL blob from the home path. The hero is text-forward in the current aesthetic; no canvas, no `HeroBlobCanvas`/`CanvasLoader`/`FallbackPoster` on the homepage render path.
- **D-06:** Sections present top-to-bottom (per ROADMAP §17.1 success criteria):
  1. **Hero** — "Founder of Prometheus" + a one-liner + ONE woven "way in" to Monty Monthly (not a button).
  2. **What I'm building** — Prometheus + current work; any invitation to connect is woven into the prose inline ("I'm building X, let's talk"), not a CTA button.
  3. **Selected work / portfolio teaser** — links into `/projects` (the real portfolio lands in 17.3).
  4. **Things I love teaser** — links into `/uses` (reframed in 17.2).
  5. **Writing + Monty Monthly gate** — Monty Monthly is the headline + subscribe action; a flagship essay or two shown OPEN as proof-of-thinking.
  6. **Footer contact** — understated; email + socials woven in.

### Engagement model (velvet rope)
- **D-07:** NO "Contact" page and NO CTA button anywhere on the homepage. Invitations to connect are soft, woven, contextual. The vibe is tension/exclusivity — "this person is very cool, how do I reach them" — and reaching Monty is deliberately not easy. Restraint over eagerness.
- **D-08:** Scarcity/selective cues are welcome ("currently building…", selective framing) but understated — never salesy.
- **D-09:** Monty Monthly's light subscribe gate IS the velvet rope — it doubles as the primary way to interface with Monty. Reply-to-newsletter, email, and socials are the understated engagement channels.

### Content mechanics
- **D-10:** Homepage copy stays hardcoded JSX in the section components (existing D-10 convention: the homepage fetches no Notion data; `revalidate = false`). Reuse/adapt the existing `section-building`, `section-writing`, `section-newsletter`, `section-footer` components rather than building from scratch where they fit the new arc.
- **D-11:** Sole professional identity is "Founder of Prometheus." Site copy rules (carried from v1): no em dashes, no location, "Georgetown University" only. Honor these in all homepage copy.

### Build/verify approach
- **D-12:** Monty is design-driven and reacts to what he SEES. The build must be shown in `npm run dev` (localhost:3000) so Monty can iterate on the real page. Prefer small visible increments over a big-bang reveal. Verification for this phase is Monty seeing the homepage render the arc with no blob.

### Claude's Discretion
- Exact section component decomposition (new files vs. adapting the existing four `section-*` components), internal layout, motion/scroll treatment within the locked aesthetic, and which existing hero pattern (`hero-cinematic`, `hero-podium`) to adapt or whether to build a fresh text hero.
- How the "woven way in" to Monty Monthly is phrased and placed in the hero.

</decisions>
