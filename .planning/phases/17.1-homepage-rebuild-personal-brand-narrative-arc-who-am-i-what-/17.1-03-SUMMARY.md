## Deviations from Plan

### Auto-fixed Issues

**1. [Rule 2 - D-11 Copy Correctness] Fixed pre-existing em dashes in NewsletterCarousel titles**
- **Found during:** Task 2 verification
- **Issue:** The existing `NewsletterCarousel` titles used em dashes in copy strings (`"Vol. 1 — Builders, Breakers, Believers"` etc.). D-11 requires no em dashes in any copy in these three files; the plan's acceptance criteria explicitly checks this.
- **Fix:** Replaced em dash with colon separator in all three issue titles (`"Vol. N: Title"` form)
- **Files modified:** `src/components/home/section-newsletter.tsx`
- **Commit:** f5f0343

**2. [Rule 2 - T-17.1-04 Security] Standalone external link for Prometheus**
- **Found during:** Task 1 implementation (anticipated by plan)
- **Issue:** BigList's `Item` type does not expose `target` or `rel` props; passing an external `prometheus.today` href through BigList would silently omit `noopener noreferrer`.
- **Fix:** Rendered Prometheus as a standalone `<a>` element with BigList's exact Tailwind classes + `target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"`. BigList handles Writing and Projects (internal routes) unchanged.
- **Files modified:** `src/components/home/section-building.tsx`
- **Commit:** 5057fb9
